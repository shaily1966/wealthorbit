About

An options education business built on published process rather than published performance.

What we do

WealthOrbit teaches options to Canadian self-directed investors, and publishes a forward record of every A+ idea our system generates — including the ones that stop out.

The course covers what an option is through to how to size a position and evaluate your own results, and finishes with a session on Canadian accounts and tax that no American course offers.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell signals or tell anyone what to trade
  • We do not promise profitability, and the course says so in its first session
  • We do not publish selected results or hide the trades that went badly
  • We are not registered to give securities advice, and we do not give it

How the ideas are published

Every idea our system grades A+ is published, without exception, before the outcome is known. Ideas are never removed, never edited after publication, and never selected for inclusion based on how they performed.

The page shows what that record does and does not demonstrate, including its limitations. That is the point of it.
